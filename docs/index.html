Abstract

A standard way to reduce hallucination in retrieval-augmented generation is to score each retrieved chunk and drop the ones that fail. We show this cannot work for multi-hop questions, explain why, and measure what does. Per-chunk scoring assumes each chunk is a sufficient premise for the answer. Multi-hop questions are built so that none is, and the paragraph carrying the answer is the one the question does not name. Entailment scoring reaches 0.951 AUC on single-hop SQuAD and 0.643, 0.523 and 0.560 on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultihopQA and MuSiQue. Seven controls rule out model capacity, premise length, hypothesis template, decision threshold, retriever, answer-matching criterion and prompt. End to end across three datasets, three generator sizes and two prompts, per-chunk gating is significantly worse than not filtering at all in every cell, and its penalty grows with generator capability. Conditioning the hypothesis on the decomposed sub-question instead of the original query lifts entailment on a later hop from 0.546, which is chance, to 0.840, a paired lift of +0.355 [0.331, 0.382].

Figure 2  ·  the repair

Ask about the right hop

Condition the hypothesis on the decomposed sub-question instead of the original query. Nothing else changes: same model, same paragraphs, same labels.

Hop count then stops mattering. The gold sub-question gives 0.848, 0.849 and 0.804 at two, three and four hops, flat within its intervals.

Self-Ask and IRCoT already produce these decompositions, for retrieval, and discard them before verifying.

negative results

What didn't work

These cost the same to produce as the positive results, and they are the part most papers leave out.

  • Holographic Reduced Representations reach 0.620 AUC. Our extractor binds 4 role-filler pairs out of 41.9 available; binding all of them drives the recoverable signal to k^-1/2 ≈ 0.15. Squeezed from both sides.
  • Our own conditional selector recovers 7 to 15 points of gold recall at 40% of the verification cost, and is significantly worse than no filtering on MuSiQue: -6.6 EM, p = .023.
  • No deployable selector beats leaving retrieval alone, on any dataset. Only the oracle does.
  • An earlier version of this study evaluated on ten questions and reported 30 to 40% Exact Match at 0.5B. The 500-question measurement puts the true value near 10%. The repository records what was decided when, and does not pretend a pre-registration existed.
  • A bug in our own measurement code forced every gold sub-question into a relational template, turning natural-language ones into meta-statements about an answer. The measured AUC fell from 0.840 to 0.779. That is the same out-of-distribution effect this paper documents, appearing in our own tooling.

Verification
without sufficiency

Per-chunk filtering fails on multi-hop RAG. Not because the models are weak, but because no single chunk is a sufficient premise for the answer.

02

Seven things it isn't

Each control rules out one competing explanation. Together they leave sufficiency as the account standing.

  1. Not one dataset0.643, 0.523 and 0.560 on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultihopQA and MuSiQue
  2. Not diffuse weaknessthe deficit vanishes on comparison questions, which name both entities: +0.271 → +0.002
  3. Not shallowembedding AUC falls 0.819 → 0.677 from two hops to four
  4. Not premise lengthlonger premises score lower: 148 words → 0.540, 198 words → 0.025
  5. Not the taskthe identical pipeline reaches 0.951 on single-hop SQuAD
  6. Not the thresholdat the most permissive cutoff tested, 84% of gold paragraphs are already rejected
  7. Not the retrieverthe deficit holds for bge, e5 and gte: +0.116, +0.107, +0.150

Also ruled out: NLI model scale (44M, 184M, 435M), the answer-matching criterion (deficit moves by ≤ 0.007 across three definitions), and the generation prompt (ordering unchanged).

03

What repairs it

Ask the verifier about the sub-question instead of the original query and the task becomes tractable. Nothing else changes: same model, same paragraphs, same labels.

Hop count then stops mattering. With the gold sub-question the AUC is 0.848, 0.849 and 0.804 at two, three and four hops, flat within its intervals.

Self-Ask and IRCoT already produce these decompositions, for retrieval, and discard them before verifying.

04

What it costs

Across three datasets, three generator sizes and two prompts, per-chunk gating is significantly worse than not filtering at all in every cell.

The penalty grows with generator capability. A stronger model is hurt more by deleted evidence, because it could have used it.

05

What didn't work

These cost the same to produce as the positive results, and they are the part most papers leave out.

  • Holographic Reduced Representations reach 0.620 AUC. Our extractor binds 4 role-filler pairs out of 41.9 available; binding all of them drives the recoverable signal to k−1/2 ≈ 0.15. Squeezed from both sides.
  • Our own conditional selector recovers 7 to 15 points of gold recall at 40% of the verification cost, and is significantly worse than no filtering on MuSiQue (−6.6 EM, p = .023).
  • No deployable selector beats leaving retrieval alone, on any dataset. Only the oracle does.
  • An earlier version of this study evaluated on ten questions and reported 30–40% Exact Match at 0.5B. The 500-question measurement puts the true value near 10%. The repository records what was decided when, and does not pretend a pre-registration existed.
